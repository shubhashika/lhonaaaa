01 About

I finished school in Kathmandu and joined the Computer Engineering programme because I liked that it sat halfway between wiring and writing code. So far the semesters have been mostly mathematics, C programming, and digital logic, with a digital logic lab that has taught me more about patience than about circuits. Outside of coursework I'm slowly teaching myself web development, one broken layout at a time.

This site itself is a class-adjacent project: hand-written HTML and CSS, no framework, no build step, just to see how far plain markup can go.
